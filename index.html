About the lab

Atomic insight.
Materials with purpose.

Materials function emerges from atomic structure and its evolution under operating conditions. At CCML, we learn interatomic interactions, guide structure searches and predict properties in close connection with first-principles theory.

Our research spans dynamic surfaces and interfaces, catalytic activity and stability, and electronic, spin, ionic and optical responses. We seek principles that connect atomic-scale mechanisms to the design of functional materials.
