Common questions

Do I need coding or computational chemistry experience?

No. Prior experience is not required. Students interested in graduate study and research are welcome to inquire; share your background and interests so we can discuss participation.

How long should undergraduate research last?

We recommend around six months to experience the research topics and methods and consider graduate study. The start date and detailed schedule can be discussed individually.

What if I have not chosen a research topic?

Browse our research directions and selected publications, and tell us what interests you or what you would like to understand. A fully developed research proposal is not needed for an initial inquiry.

Where should I visit?

The laboratory is in Wonheung Hall E243 and E244. The PI office is in Manhae Hall B211. Please email first to arrange a meeting.
