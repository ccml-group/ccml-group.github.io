# Journal Impact Factor verification

Independently rechecked 2026-10-26: all 37 works reviewed; the 30 published and 3 accepted works have 33 JIF records, and 4 submitted works correctly have none. All 33 raw values and assigned JIF citation years agree with the checked sources; no numeric IF corrections were needed.

## Display and policy

Published works use the latest two-year Clarivate JIF publicly available at first online publication. Accepted works use the latest available JIF as of the check date. Values are not CiteScore or five-year JIF. The displayed badge rounds half up to exactly one decimal place. Original precision is retained in `data/publications.json`; the representative-paper eligibility threshold IF ≥ 9 uses that unrounded value.

## Verification method and limitations

- Re-read the public JCR metric tables and publisher records cited per work below. Older publisher metrics were cross-checked with university/library journal histories. Public JCR exports are hosted by libraries/research institutes; this audit did not use an authenticated JCR subscription.
- Requeried all 30 published DOIs at Crossref. 29 returned metadata; #26 returned HTTP 429, so its April 2026 date was checked against the publisher’s public article metadata instead. No publication-date discrepancy affecting an assigned JIF year was found.
- Crossref gives year-only dates for several RSC papers; #1, #3, #17, #21 and #28 were checked against RSC first-publication metadata. #29 now has a complete Crossref online date (2026-07-25). #30 is confirmed to July 2026 by the coauthor’s Stanford bibliography; this is sufficient to select 2025 JIF, but is not an independently verified first-online day.
- #12 has a March 2023 issue date and November 2022 DOI-registration metadata. Its exact first-online day remains unconfirmed. Both candidate periods select 2021 JIF; DOI registration is not presented as an exact publication date.
- JCR 2026 export columns are release years: its “JIF 2026” is citation-year 2025; “JIF 2025” is citation-year 2024. This was cross-checked against the RSC page explicitly reporting 9.2 (2025), the ACS current metrics and the 2024 JCR export.
- Checked the October correction notices for JCR 2024, 2025 and 2026. The 2023 JIF corrections JACS 14.4 → 14.5 and JMCA 10.7 → 10.8 were released 2024-10-23, before #18 and #21. The 2026-10-14 notice has no changes for accepted AEM, ACS Nano or Chem Catalysis; their latest values remain 25.5, 17.3 and 10.7. The 2025-10-15 correction notice does not change the applicable 2024 ACS Catalysis, ACS AMI or JMCA values. It changes JACS 2024 JIF from 15.6 to 15.7, but #24 first appeared on 2025-08-13, before that correction, so 15.6 remains the correct publication-time value.
