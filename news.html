2026.11.02Publication

Polymer electrolyte study published in Advanced Energy Materials

A collaborative study on zwitterionic thermoplastic polyurethane-based quasi-solid polymer electrolytes for lithium metal batteries is now online in Advanced Energy Materials. The work includes machine-learning-assisted insights into the electrolyte mechanisms.

2026.10.27Publication

Cu–Pd cluster catalysis study published in ACS Nano

A collaborative study of selective hydrogen release on Cu–Pd pentamer clusters is now online in ACS Nano. The work investigates oxygen-assisted dehydrogenation and the reaction mechanisms of zirconia-supported clusters.

Read the paper ↗
2026.07.25Publication

Grain-boundary stability study published in Nature Communications

A collaborative study in Nature Communications shows how grain-boundary chemistry can improve the chemical stability of yttrium telluride thermoelectric materials. First-principles calculations explain Bi segregation and its role in suppressing moisture-induced degradation.

Read the paper ↗
2026.07.01Research funding

Collaborative project under the Creative Materials Discovery Plus Program begins

CCML joins the Hanyang University-led project “CONDUCT-Based Structure-Expanding Inverse Design for Asymmetric Circularly Polarized Luminescent Superstructures,” supported by the Ministry of Science and ICT and the National Research Foundation of Korea. The lab contributes first-principles calculations and machine-learning approaches to structure–property analysis and materials inverse design.

2026.03.01Research funding

NRF Young Scientist Grant (Type A) project begins

CCML has started “AI-Driven Multiscale Modeling for Long-Term Sintering Prediction and Stability Analysis of Rh/TiO₂ Catalysts” under the Young Scientist Grants (Type A). Supported by the Ministry of Science and ICT and the National Research Foundation of Korea, the project studies catalyst sintering and long-term stability through AI-driven multiscale modeling.
