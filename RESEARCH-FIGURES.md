# Research figure provenance

Updated 25 November 2026 (upper atomistic simulation panels and fourth research figure replaced). The four overview images are stored in `assets/research/` and used on Home and Research. Research also links to each full-size image. These are 1600 × 1600 PNG collages using original research figures and explanatory diagrams. Published atomic structures and numerical results remain original source panels, not generative recreations.

Panels were cropped, resized proportionally and placed on white canvases. Old panel letters and redundant snapshot indices were removed from blank margins. Scientific structure identifiers linking curves to their atomic models remain intact. Existing atomic structures, scientific curves, chemical labels and reaction arrows were retained. New headings communicate the role of learning and prediction. The atomistic simulation overview pairs method schematics with actual research results. Sources and the status of the design workflow are recorded here.

The upper atomistic panels illustrate foundation model fine-tuning and structure search. The poster’s energy landscape is conceptual. The two lower TiO₂ panels retain the original graph axes, units, curves, structure identifiers and compositions.

The interface overview separates experimental TiO₂ reconstruction from BiVO₄ aqueous-interface simulations. In the TiO₂ micrographs, only the upper background strip containing panel letters and timestamps was cropped. Source times (0, 28 and 34 s) are repeated below each image. Atomic contrast, red arrows, green position guides and 1 nm scale bars are preserved. The BiVO₄ direct and indirect reaction snapshots have equal display sizes. Coordination-state and water-activation diagrams remain unchanged in scientific content; the aqueous panels are not relabeled as ML trajectories. The catalyst graphic shows the published data-driven hybrid model without asserting a neural-network architecture.




Publisher full-text/image access was checked on the user's authorized MacBook browser. The 20 October TiO₂ check returned abstract-only access on the Mac mini, while the authorized MacBook browser showed Dongguk University Seoul Campus and Full Access. Figures 2 and 3 were obtained individually through the publisher figure viewer. Previously saved graphical abstracts, author-preprint PDF and other original panels were reused. Original publications retain their respective authorship and copyright. This document records provenance and edits; access alone is not a reuse license.

Some original raster panels are relatively small; their native detail limits enlargement. No generative upscaling or redrawing was applied to the original publication panels.
