# Research figure provenance

Updated 20 October 2026 (pictorial ML revision). The four overview images are stored in `assets/research/` and used on Home and Research. Research also links to each full-size image. These are 1600 × 1600 PNG collages of existing publication panels with explanatory workflows and a new conceptual illustration. Published atomic structures and numerical results remain original source panels, not generative recreations.

Panels were cropped, resized proportionally and placed on white canvases. Old panel letters and circled panel indices were removed from their blank margins. Existing atomic structures, scientific curves, chemical labels and reaction arrows were retained. New headings and explanatory visuals communicate the role of learning and prediction. The Functional Materials Design overview replaces its box-and-arrow workflow with a large pictorial ML scene. Sources are recorded here rather than printed inside the overview images.

The Fe₄N DOS panels show hydrogen at surface (upper) and bulk (lower) octahedral sites. The RuO₂ Wulff shapes are examples at different oxygen chemical potentials, not a kinetic time sequence. The separate material examples in a collage do not imply a single combined material or experiment.

The active-learning workflow is a schematic of selecting new first-principles training data during structure exploration. The interface workflow explicitly separates ML-guided surface exploration from subsequent DFT refinement and interface simulation; the aqueous reaction panels are not relabeled as ML trajectories. The catalyst graphic shows the published data-driven hybrid model without asserting a neural-network architecture.

The PI confirmed on 20 October 2026 that ML property prediction and candidate exploration are current research activities in functional materials design, and requested an original illustration where published figures are unavailable. The new materials illustration depicts abstract atomic neighborhoods, learned representations and candidate motifs over a conceptual landscape. It was made with the built-in image-generation tool and placed above the four original paper panels using deterministic composition. Its atoms, network and landscape are illustrative, not a particular compound, model architecture, calculated property surface, reported benchmark or newly discovered structure. The older WO₃/Fe₄N panels are not represented as ML outputs. No accuracy scores or numerical predictions were added.

Publisher full-text/image access was checked on the user's authorized MacBook browser. New source images were obtained through publisher figure views; previously saved local graphical abstracts were reused. Original publications retain their respective authorship and copyright. This document records provenance and edits; access alone is not a reuse license.

Some original raster panels are relatively small; their native detail limits enlargement. No generative upscaling or redrawing was applied to the original publication panels. Only the separate conceptual ML illustration was generated. The previous artistic illustrations remain in `assets/` for recovery but are no longer displayed on Home or Research.
